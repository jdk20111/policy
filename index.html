Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Terms of Service

This policy covers the small, self-hosted applications I run for my own household, including a Gmail spam filter and home-automation integrations (together, "the apps"). They are private tools, not a commercial service. They are not offered to the public and are used only with accounts I own or manage for my household.

What the apps access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the apps may access only the data needed for the feature you authorized. For example:

  • Gmail: message headers (sender, recipient, subject, date, authentication results), a short excerpt of the message text, and labels. The apps use them to decide whether a message is spam or unwanted bulk mail.
  • Sending mail: when a feature is set up to send you a notification or a list by email, the apps compose and send that message from your account.

How the data is used

  • To sort incoming mail: adding labels such as "Probably spam", and, only if configured, moving mail to Spam or archiving it. The apps never delete mail.
  • To send messages you asked for, such as notifications.
  • For nothing else: no advertising, profiling, or resale, and no training of machine-learning models.

Automated processing

To classify a message, the spam filter sends the headers and short excerpt listed above to Anthropic's Claude model, using the owner's own Claude subscription. The data goes there only to get a classification back. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that processing.

Storage and retention

  • Data is stored on a privately owned server in my home that isn't exposed to the internet. No third-party hosting or database is used.
  • The spam filter keeps a decision log: sender, subject, verdict, and a short reason. It does not store message bodies.
  • OAuth tokens are stored on that server with file permissions restricting access, and are included in encrypted backups.
  • Logs are kept until deleted by the owner. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing. It is shared only as described under "Automated processing", or where the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • You can revoke the apps' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, they can no longer read or change your data.
  • You can ask for any stored data about you to be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in (consent) screen.
